## 2. Privacy

- The app never asks for a name or a contact. Free text a guest types is redacted for emails and phone numbers (international, national and Gambian formats) before anything is stored, once ingest exists.
- Noor's monthly text is a checked template filled with counts and checked theme labels, and nothing else.
- The outbox schema is strict: an unknown field is rejected, so no personal field can ride along.
- Audio of people's voices is never in git (the repository is public). Teammates get it privately; `content/ondera-noor/recordings/audio.sha256` lets them verify the files.
- The 116 test questions contain no personal data (a test redacts them and expects no change).

## 4. Human oversight

What exists today: the `checks.json` mechanism and the pack gating. **By design, not built:** the guest's confirm card, Noor's held report and reviewer approval, and the order confirmation screen.

- **Checks:** nothing is checked until a person signs an entry in `checks.json` (who, when). A subtitle check is bound to the exact text it approved: new or regenerated text makes it a draft again.
- **The guest decides:** the phone shows a topic label and the guest confirms before anything plays; below the threshold the question is saved for Noor.
- **Noor's text:** held until a Wolof speaker checks the template and a reviewer approves (to be built).
- **Orders:** counted only after Noor confirms payment in person (schema enforced; the screen is to be built).

## 5. Honest trade-offs

- **The threshold.** `docs/EVAL.md` reports the number on held-out halves of the questions: about 5% false confirmations (interval 2% to 11%), about 52% coverage. One fold alone was at 9.6%. The shipped threshold is the strictest of three picks, which trades coverage for caution. A false confirmation shows a wrong card the guest can reject; it never plays unconfirmed.
- **Synthetic questions.** Written by the team, not by guests; de, nl and sv unchecked. Real guests will differ.
- **Machine drafts and non-commercial models.** NLLB and MMS are CC-BY-NC; their output is labeled and never shipped to production unchecked.
- **Wolof.** Machine translation and speech recognition for Wolof are expected to be weak but **have not been measured**; the runs are planned (Slice 4 step 4). The dubbed clips are synthetic speech. A Wolof speaker has to check before anything reaches Noor.
- **A stand-in voice.** Preet's voice stands in for Noor's, disclosed on screen in every language. It is honest but it is not Noor.
- **Index-only passages (planned, Slice 4 step 2, not built):** unchecked question-style phrasings that would never be displayed but would affect which clip is offered. The captain decided on 2026-10-04 (recorded in `tasks/todo.md`) that they may ship in production, labeled in the manifest; that is in tension with rule 5 and is flagged here on purpose. The guest still confirms every match.
- **Dropped parts.** To fit two SMS segments the monthly text can drop its least important parts; the reviewer must see which.
- **Different CPUs, slightly different vectors.** The threshold has not been checked against phone-made vectors.
