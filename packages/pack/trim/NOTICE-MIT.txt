Ask Noor: trimmed multilingual-e5-small (int8 ONNX)

  Original model:  https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small  (authors: intfloat)
  ONNX int8 build: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/multilingual-e5-small  (revision 761b726dd34fb83930e26aab4e9ac3899aa1fa78)

What we changed: we cut the rows of the word-embedding table (and the matching tokenizer entries) that
Ask Noor does not need, keeping 52,005 of 250,002. The kept rows are byte-identical to the original
int8 rows. Nothing else changed. The recipe and the kept-id list are in the Ask Noor repository
(packages/pack/trim/keep-ids.json, model-trimmed.lock.json).
